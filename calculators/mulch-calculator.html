How to use this mulch calculator

Measure the length and width of each bed in feet. For round beds, use the widest diameter in both dimensions - the small corner loss is covered by your depth choice.

Pick a depth: 2 inches for a refreshing layer over existing mulch, 3 inches (recommended) for weed suppression, or 4 inches for new beds with heavy weeds.

One cubic yard covers roughly 108 sq ft at 3 inches deep. In bags, a 2 cu ft bag covers about 8 sq ft at 3 inches - so plan to grab 12-14 bags for a typical 100 sq ft bed.

Mulch calculator FAQ

How much mulch do I need for 100 square feet?

At the recommended 3-inch depth, 100 sq ft needs 25 cu ft of mulch: about 13 bags of 2 cu ft, or just under one cubic yard.

How many square feet does a yard of mulch cover?

One cubic yard of mulch covers about 162 sq ft at 2 inches deep, 108 sq ft at 3 inches, and 81 sq ft at 4 inches.

Should I buy mulch in bags or bulk?

Bags are better for small beds and make spreading easier. For anything over 2 cubic yards, bulk delivery is usually 30-50% cheaper - check your supplier's per-yard price.
