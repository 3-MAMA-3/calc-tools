How to use this carpet calculator

Measure the room length and width in feet. Carpet is sold in 12-foot-wide rolls, so the width of your room determines how much is wasted as trimmings.

If either dimension exceeds 12 feet, the installer must seam two pieces - add one extra foot to the length for every seam to allow pattern matching and trimming.

Carpet is quoted in square yards: divide your square footage by 9. Always order 5-10% extra, because installers cannot splice a small missing piece invisibly.

Carpet calculator FAQ

How many square yards is a 12x12 room?

A 12x12 room is 144 sq ft, which is exactly 16 square yards of carpet before waste. With a plain carpet and clean seams, 16-18 sq yd is a safe order.

Why do carpet installers charge for waste?

Carpet comes in 12-foot-wide rolls, so a 14x12 room needs a 14-foot length of a 12-foot-wide roll: 168 sq ft of carpet for a 144 sq ft floor. That off-cut is still charged.

How much does carpet cost per square foot?

Installed carpet typically runs $3-$8 per sq ft in 2025-2026: $2-$4 for materials and $1.50-$3 for installation, padding and removal of the old carpet.
