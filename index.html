Why use material calculators?

Every renovation project has one common failure: buying too little and stopping mid-project, or buying too much and wasting money. Each calculator here uses standard industry coverage rates - 350 sq ft per gallon of paint, 27 cu ft per cubic yard of concrete, 10% flooring waste - so your estimate matches what contractors calculate.

All tools are instant, free, and work on any device. Every result generates a shareable link (your inputs are encoded in the URL), so you can send your estimate to a contractor or save it for your next trip to the store.

Frequently asked questions

Are these calculators accurate?

They use standard industry coverage rates used by professional painters, concrete suppliers and flooring installers. Always buy 5-10% extra for waste, and confirm unusual conditions with a supplier.

Do I need an app or account?

No. Everything runs in your browser instantly - no signup, no download, no tracking beyond basic analytics.

Can I save my results?

Yes - every result page includes a shareable link that reproduces your exact inputs and results. Bookmark it or send it to yourself.
